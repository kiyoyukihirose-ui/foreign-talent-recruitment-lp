Interview with an
International
Team Member

An international team member at ARIA is already working in Business Intelligence / Internal Consulting.
We asked him about his Japanese level when he joined ARIA and his experience working here.

Q1. How much Japanese could you speak when you joined ARIA?

I think my Japanese was around JLPT N3 level.
At that time, I had not taken the JLPT yet.

Q2. Has Japanese been difficult when working at ARIA?

Of course. I still have difficulties with Japanese sometimes.
But everyone at ARIA is very kind. If I cannot find the right words in Japanese, nobody rushes me or expects me to pretend that I understand. I can ask questions and confirm things as we work, so I feel comfortable working here.

Q3. How do you work with the CEO and business leaders?

I can speak directly with the CEO and business leaders about problems I want to improve and ideas I have.
Of course, not every idea is accepted. But if there is a clear reason behind it, people listen seriously. If the idea is good, it can become real work.

Q4. What has been one of your most memorable experiences at ARIA?

One of my connections overseas led to an opportunity to work with a company outside Japan.
Instead of just discussing the idea, we formed a temporary project team and traveled overseas to move the project forward.
If your experience, knowledge, or connections can create value for the company, ARIA is willing to use them and take action. That is one of the things I find interesting about working here.
