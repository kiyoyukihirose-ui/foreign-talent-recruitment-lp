What You Will
Work On

ARIA operates several businesses in different fields, including legal services, online buyback, and web marketing.

Here are some examples of real business topics we work on at ARIA.

Legal Services

  • Analyze CRM data to evaluate profitability by case
  • Analyze the funnel from inquiry to consultation, contract, and payment to improve client communication and follow up
  • Track results through consultation, contract, and payment to evaluate the quality of each acquisition channel
  • Use CRM, AI, and automation tools to design systems for case management and progress tracking

Online Buyback

  • Analyze repeat usage and transaction history to identify high LTV customers and usage patterns
  • Track and evaluate customer value by acquisition channel
  • Use customer activity history to design LINE targeting, offers, and message timing
  • Design bonus programs to encourage repeat use and measure their results

After joining, you will work with an international team member already at ARIA to identify problems through business data and day to day operations, and improve them.

You will first work on existing businesses. Based on your results, you may later take part in market analysis and business hypothesis development for new businesses.
